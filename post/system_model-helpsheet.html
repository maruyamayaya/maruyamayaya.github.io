Choosing a formula by what is measured

A person picked at random and a person reached by following a friendship have different degree distributions. A system updated once per day and one evolving continuously have different stability boundaries. Choose the population first, then the sampling rule and the time scale. A change in any of them can alter which average or threshold describes the system.
